Delete your PlateRate account

You can delete your account in two ways:

  1. In the app (fastest): open PlateRate → Settings → delete account.
  2. On the web: email us using the details below (required for store policies if you cannot use the app).

What deletion removes (as implemented in our systems): your login, profile data, notifications, blocks, location-sharing data, vehicle/fleet links you own, and content you authored, along with related votes/flags where applicable. Some residual technical logs may be kept for a limited time for security or legal reasons.

Request by email

Send an email to Pla8r8@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Account deletion request
  • The email address registered on the PlateRate account
  • Optional: any extra detail to help us find the account

We will reply to confirm and process verified requests within a reasonable time (and within any legal deadlines).

Optional form (opens your email app)

Fill this in, then tap the button. It prepares an email to us — it does not delete anything by itself until we process your request.

This page is part of PlateRate’s account-deletion process described in our Privacy Policy.
